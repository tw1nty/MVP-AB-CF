# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students reviewing their degree plan and deciding whether to accept recommended course swaps.

## Product Purpose

Pathway helps students compare alternatives and understand the combined effect of accepted changes on cost and graduation. This project is a visual, interactive redesign of the supplied ClassFindr screenshots, using illustrative data rather than a live academic calculation service.

## Brand Personality

Clear, compact, trustworthy. Preserve ClassFindr's blue branding, as confirmed by the user.

## Anti-references

The supplied screens have excessive whitespace, oversized introductory panels, and scattered summary information. Avoid repeating degree information and making students manually add up swap impacts.

## Design Principles

- Keep course comparisons close to the summary they affect.
- Separate accepted impact from proposed opportunities.
- Present all four optimization strategies as a guided single-select question, with an explicit Continue step.
- Explain the benefits and tradeoffs of the selected strategy in a paragraph beneath the choices.
- Keep cost savings and estimated graduation together on the right of the unified priority section; course and credit counts are excluded from that summary.
- Keep graduation assumptions visible; do not invent a broader definition of time saved.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use visible labels, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, and text alongside color indicators. Respect reduced-motion preferences. These are implementation defaults rather than a claim of certified compliance.
